# Business Case: Customer Loyalty Program for Trinidad Food

## Executive Summary

Trinidad Food has an opportunity to strengthen customer retention, increase repeat purchases, and drive revenue growth through the implementation of a customer loyalty program.

The proposed program requires an investment of **$50,000** and is projected to generate a **10% increase in annual revenue**, resulting in **$100,000 in additional revenue** during the first year. After accounting for implementation costs, the initiative is expected to deliver **$50,000 in net profit** and achieve a **100% return on investment (ROI)**.

## Business Opportunity

A loyalty program encourages customers to return more frequently, increase their spending, and build a stronger connection with the Trinidad Food brand. By rewarding repeat purchases, the company can improve customer retention while creating sustainable revenue growth.

## Financial Impact

| Metric | Amount |
|----------|----------:|
| Loyalty Program Investment | $50,000 |
| Additional Revenue (Year 1) | $100,000 |
| Net Profit | **$50,000** |
| Return on Investment (ROI) | **100%** |

## Strategic Benefits

| Benefit | Impact |
|----------|----------|
| Customer Retention | Encourages repeat visits and purchases |
| Revenue Growth | Generates additional sales from loyal customers |
| Brand Loyalty | Strengthens long-term customer relationships |
| Competitive Advantage | Differentiates Trinidad Food from competitors |

## Recommendation

Based on the projected financial return and strategic advantages, Trinidad Food should proceed with the loyalty program implementation.

### Key Reasons to Approve the Investment

- **$100,000** in projected additional revenue during the first year.
- **$50,000** in expected net profit after implementation costs.
- **100% ROI**, meaning the investment is fully recovered while generating an equivalent amount in profit.
- Improved customer retention and stronger long-term customer relationships.
- Potential for continued revenue growth beyond the first year.

## Conclusion

The loyalty program represents a low-risk, high-value investment that aligns with Trinidad Food's growth objectives. For an investment of **$50,000**, the company is projected to generate **$100,000 in additional revenue**, deliver **$50,000 in net profit**, and achieve a **100% ROI** in the first year. This initiative offers both immediate financial benefits and long-term strategic value, making it a compelling opportunity for approval.
